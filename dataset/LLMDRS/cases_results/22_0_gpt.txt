1. Include more iron-rich foods in the diet:
- Consume lean red meats, poultry, and fish that are rich in heme iron, which is more easily absorbed by the body.
- Include plant-based non-heme iron sources, such as spinach, lentils, beans, nuts, seeds, and whole grains. This type of iron is not as easily absorbed; therefore, consume these foods with vitamin C-rich foods to enhance iron absorption. Examples of vitamin C-rich foods are oranges, strawberries, bell peppers, and tomatoes.

2. Limit consumption of sweets and baked goods:
- These foods can be high in empty calories and contribute to weight gain without providing essential nutrients.
- Instead, choose healthier snack options such as fresh fruits, nuts, and yogurt.

3. Reduce or eliminate soft drinks:
- Soft drinks are high in sugar and can contribute to excessive weight gain and provide minimal nutritional value.
- Replace soft drinks with water, herbal tea, or freshly squeezed juices.

4. Choose whole grains over refined grains:
- Whole grains such as brown rice, quinoa, and whole wheat bread contain more nutrients, including iron, than their refined counterparts.

5. Incorporate more fruits and vegetables:
- Include a variety of colorful fruits and vegetables in the diet, as they provide essential vitamins, minerals, and antioxidants.
